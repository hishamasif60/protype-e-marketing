About Me

Hisham Asif, 27

Direct Response Copywriter from Abbottabad, Pakistan

I specialize in crafting persuasive copy, particularly for email marketing, designed to connect with audiences and drive action. My focus is on creating email sequences – from welcoming new subscribers to nurturing leads and closing sales.

I honed my skills under the guidance of Armash Kamal, widely recognized as Pakistan's #1 copywriter. This training provided me with a strong foundation in direct response principles that I apply across various formats, including sales pages, video sales letters (VSLs), web pages, ads, and landing pages.

Beyond writing, I'm proficient in managing Email Service Providers (ESPs) like ActiveCampaign, Systeme.io, and Mailchimp, including setting up automations and segmenting lists for targeted communication. I also have experience with cold outreach tools like Sales Handy and Contact Out.

My goal is to help businesses effectively communicate their value and achieve their marketing objectives through powerful, results-oriented copy.
